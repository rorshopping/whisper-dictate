Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

The short version

Your voice never leaves your computer. The app transcribes audio locally on your own GPU/CPU. There is no server, no telemetry, no analytics and no account.

The app

  • Audio is recorded only while you hold the hotkey and is processed on your machine.
  • Transcription runs locally using NVIDIA Nemotron open models; nothing is uploaded.
  • A local history file (transcription-history.jsonl) and log file stay on your machine; both are yours to delete.
  • The only network traffic is the one-time download of the speech models (~2.4 GB per language) from Hugging Face when you first use a language.

This website

This site is fully static: no cookies, no analytics, no tracking, no external fonts or scripts beyond what is listed in the page source.

Payments

If you buy Pro, the payment is processed by Stripe. We receive your email address and payment status from Stripe (to deliver the license and receipts) and nothing else. Stripe's own handling of payment data is governed by the Stripe Privacy Policy. Payment card details never reach us.
